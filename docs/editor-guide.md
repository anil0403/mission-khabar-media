# Editor guide — publishing news on Mission Khabar

This page explains how to log in, write news, add images and videos, and handle comments.

## 1. Log in
Go to **missionkhabarmedia.com.np/admin** and sign in with your email and password.
If you forgot your password, click **Forgot password?** on the login page.

Your role decides what you can do:
- **Reporter:** writes news and saves it as a draft. An editor publishes it.
- **Editor:** writes, publishes, and edits anyone's news. Also moderates comments.
- **Admin:** everything, plus adding staff and changing site settings.

## 2. Write a news article
1. On the left, click **Articles**, then **Create new**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title:** the headline, in Nepali or English.
   - **Excerpt:** one or two sentences. They show on the home page and when shared on Facebook.
   - **Cover:** the main photo. Click it and upload a new photo, or pick one you uploaded before. **Always fill in "Alt"** with a few words describing the photo.
   - **Body:** the full story. Use the toolbar for headings, bold text, lists and links.
     - To add a **photo** inside the story, type `/` and choose **Upload**.
     - To add a **YouTube video**, type `/`, choose **YouTube video**, and paste the link.
     - To add a **Facebook post or video**, type `/`, choose **Facebook post/video**, and paste the link.
3. In the right-hand panel:
   - **Category:** required.
   - **Breaking news:** puts the story in the red "Breaking" bar at the top of the site.
   - **Featured on home page:** makes it the big top story.
   - **Tags:** optional keywords.
   - **Slug:** the web address. Leave it empty and it's made from the title automatically.
4. Click **Save draft** to keep working later, or **Publish changes** to make it live (editors and admins only).

The story shows on the website right after publishing. You don't need to do anything else.

## 3. Edit or unpublish
- Open the article, change it, then click **Publish changes** again. The site updates immediately.
- To take a story down, click **Unpublish**. Editors can also delete it.
- If you change the slug of a published story, the old link still works: it forwards to the new one.

## 4. Google and Facebook previews (optional)
The **SEO** fields fill themselves in from the title, excerpt and cover. Only change them if you want a different headline on Google or Facebook. The preview shows how it will look.

## 5. Comments
Readers can like, comment and @mention each other. To remove a bad comment, go to **Community → Comments**, open the comment, set **Status** to **Hidden**, and save.

## 6. Site settings (admin only)
Under **Settings → Site settings** you can change:
- the tagline and social media links
- contact details, Press Council and company registration numbers, and team names (shown in the footer)
- ads: header banner, sidebar, and inside articles. Upload an image and add the link.

Pages like About, Advertise, Privacy and Contact are under **Pages**.
